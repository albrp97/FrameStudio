# TICKET-078 Cross-Model Restoration Benchmark

This report records the completed four-window benchmark against the requested
real video. It is research evidence for one fixture and one RTX 5070 Ti
workstation; it does not change the production editor or export route.

## Result

- **Candidate rows:** 24
- **Passed:** 17
- **Unavailable:** 4
- **Excluded by license:** 1
- **Not comparable:** 2
- **Failed:** 0
- **Completed output videos:** 18
- **Verified output videos:** 18
- **Contact sheets:** 18

The fastest completed model-stage measurement was native RVE
SuperUltraCompact at **186.078 FPS**. Video2X
`realesr-animevideov3` processed the padded adapter input at **79.78 FPS
reported by Video2X** and **76.22 FPS by wall time**. The heavier Video2X
`realesrgan-plus` baseline ran at **4.68 FPS reported** and **4.66 FPS by
wall time**, with substantially higher GPU utilization.

These are throughput results only. No visual quality winner is claimed until
the contact sheets and output videos are reviewed.

`acf6420455324e66d9c68d644888c3cb2977282d609d514d80c169c9918a7d2f`

The initial current-fixture Video2X invocation failed before processing because
`/usr/bin/time` is not installed; it was rerun with the shell `time` builtin.
The first audio remux used `-shortest` and produced 1,435 frames. The final
artifacts were remuxed with video/audio stream copying and without
`-shortest`, then reverified at 1,436 frames. No partial output remains.

## Review suggestions and limitations

1. Review the contact sheets at 7s, 22s, 37s, and 52s, then inspect the
   corresponding videos for faces, text, blocking, ringing, halos, invented
   detail, flicker, motion stability, and segment boundaries.
2. Start visual comparison with RVE SuperUltraCompact, RVE NAFNet, RVE SPAN,
   BasicVSR, both Video2X configurations, and the Lanczos control.
3. Treat RVE native runs separately from the 480x270 BasicVSR and Video2X
   adapter runs. The adapter results are useful operational comparisons but
   are not native-resolution rankings.
4. The low GPU utilization of `realesr-animevideov3` is consistent with its
   lighter model workload. The heavier `realesrgan-plus` run reached 83.25%
   average and 97% peak GPU utilization, so the fast run is not evidence of
   a missing model load.
5. No PSNR, SSIM, or universal quality ranking is reported because this
   real-world fixture has no clean aligned reference.
